# Design Direction

## Product character

Warm, trustworthy, local and technology-enabled. ApnaServo should feel premium without becoming ornamental or generic.

## System

- Warm ivory canvas, white elevated surfaces, near-black text, muted neutral copy and restrained rose accents.
- Manrope for display hierarchy and Inter for supporting text.
- Radius scale: 12px controls, 20px cards, 28px feature media.
- Two restrained shadow levels and warm neutral borders.
- Fluid type with controlled line lengths and consistent 8px-derived spacing.
- Motion only for feedback and gentle state changes; reduced-motion disables nonessential movement.
- One shared navigation language, one focus-ring system and consistent 44px minimum controls.

## Homepage narrative

Promise → proof → services → verification → app product → regional launch → footer. Existing content and business meaning remain intact.
