# Final Visual Review

## Result

The final layer establishes a consistent warm-ivory/rose system, shared typography, radii, shadows, focus treatment and responsive rhythm without replacing the existing application or its content.

## Major improvements

- Homepage hierarchy is now hero → proof → services → verification → app → regional launch on mobile.
- Hero remains a premium blended text/video composition on desktop, becomes landscape on tablet and 16:9 on mobile.
- Cards, buttons, legal surfaces, support options and footer links now share a restrained component language.
- Support and policy layouts use readable mobile typography, single-column narrow-screen composition and larger controls.
- Services small-mobile cards become readable one-column content rather than compressed tiles.

## Visual QA evidence

- Baseline browser audits covered 1440×900, 768×1024 and 390×844.
- Final browser automation was attempted at all seven required sizes, but the in-app browser repeatedly timed out during navigation after the production build. No reliable final screenshot files were produced; this is recorded as a remaining QA limitation rather than represented as completed evidence.
- Static CSS review confirms explicit handling for large desktop, laptop, tablet, large mobile and small mobile.
